Creatures that evolve, measurably: readers of damaged FallKard seal codes, bred, battled and selected against real model misreads, re-runnable to the byte.

# kard-evolve

**Live: https://sjgant80-hub.github.io/kard-evolve/**

The first step of the FallKard creatures vision, built by Kar. A creature here is not a picture of a thing; it is a
working reader. Its genes are rules you can read: "read X as Y" folds, plus how many corrections it will accept, how far
ahead of the runner-up the best card must be, and how many unreadable symbols it tolerates. Its fitness is measured, not
assigned: every creature resolves damaged seal codes against the real deck, graded by rules. Creatures battle, winners
breed through a birth gate, children mutate, the best survive. Nothing in the code favours any particular gene.

The world is real. The 192 replies from [fallkard-forge](https://sjgant80-hub.github.io/fallkard-forge/)'s sealed paid
read, where a vision model read printed seal codes off card pictures, are the damaged codes. The first 32 cards train and
the last 32 are held out; selection never sees a held-out card. The deliberate damage added to both halves is drawn only
from the slips measured on the training cards.

**Sealed first.** `data/prereg.json` fixed the world's and the kernel's sha256, the configuration, five rules and a
prediction before the first generation. A made-up pilot world is disclosed there, including the design change it forced.

## Experiment 2 · the creature that watches itself

A creature that looks at its own misreads, with no answer key, and rewrites its own genes from them, against blind mutation and against blind search given the same compute and self-scoring, on the same world, with 8 seeds. Sealed first in `data/observe-prereg.json`; page: [observer.html](https://sjgant80-hub.github.io/kard-evolve/observer.html).

**Experiment 2 — the creature that watches itself: 6 of 6 sealed rules held.** With no answer key, only what it can see about itself, the observer found U → V at a median generation of 1; blind trials with the same compute and self-scoring took 1.5; blind mutation took 6.5. [The page](https://sjgant80-hub.github.io/kard-evolve/observer.html) has its diary and a re-run button.

How the speed-up splits: scoring changes by its own confidence (blind mutation → blind trials) moved the median 5 generations sooner; watching its own misreads (blind trials → observer) moved it a further 0.5, with less compute than the control. Every one of the 24 champions ended equally fit on the held-out cards: the observer gets there first, not further. On seed 5 a random newborn already carried U → V in generation 0, for every arm.

## Run it

```bash
node --test                    # the kernel's tests
node tools/evolve.mjs --verify # re-run the whole evolution from the seal; exits 1 unless byte-identical
node tools/pilot.mjs --check   # re-run the pilot on its made-up world
node tools/make-page.mjs       # regenerate the page, README and llms.txt from the committed data
```

Or open the live page and press "Re-run the evolution": the same kernel, in your browser, in about a minute.

## Gate

`evolve.mjs` is pure and mutation-gated clean, with nothing baselined. CI runs the tests, the pinned witness, the page
fixpoint, the seal check, the full evolution re-run and the pilot re-run on every push. `vendor/sealmark.mjs` is
fallkard-forge's gated kernel, vendored byte-exact from commit d672d84, and its sha256 is sealed.
